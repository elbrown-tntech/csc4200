## 4. Why these licenses

The commercial textbook was eliminated from this course to remove a cost
barrier for students and to allow the material to be corrected as standards
change. Licensing the material openly is the same decision carried through:
other institutions — particularly other NCAE-C designated programs — are
welcome to use and adapt it.

The NonCommercial term is there to prevent the material being repackaged and
sold back to students, which would undo the reason the textbook was dropped in
the first place. It is not intended to restrict use by other educators. If you
are at an institution where the NonCommercial term is an obstacle to legitimate
educational use, contact elbrown@tntech.edu — permission is likely, and
straightforward to grant.

The code is under MIT rather than a Creative Commons license because CC
licenses do not address patent rights, source distribution, or linking, and are
not designed for software. MIT is permissive, short, and widely understood.
